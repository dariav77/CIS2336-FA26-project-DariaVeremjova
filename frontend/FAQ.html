Frequenty Asked Questions

1. What are student center hours?

The student center hours are from 8:00am to 9:00pm everyday.

2. Who can use the student center?

Study rooms and student center spaces are primarily available to current students, faculty, and staff of the University of Houston. Student organizations and department staff may also book rooms for official university activities through the online reservation portal.

3. How do I book a room?

To book a room please, navigate the the "Room Booking" tile above, and fill out the required information.

4. How long can I reserve a room?

One person is able to reserve a room for up to 2 hours a day.

5. What should I do if my room is unavailable?

If another group is occupying your reserved room, politely show them your booking confirmation email or digital pass. If the room remains occupied, please check the digital display outside the door or visit the front desk/library help desk immediately. Staff will help relocate you to an available alternative space and resolve the scheduling conflict.
